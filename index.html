About

From flying robots to walking ones

I spent three and a half years building autonomous drones with Team UAS-DTU, first as an engineer and team pilot, then as Avionics Head leading 20+ people across perception, planning and control. We podiumed at three international competitions, including the DARPA Triage Challenge in Georgia.

Along the way I moved from classical pipelines toward learned controllers: PPO policies flying quadrotors on a Jetson at IIIT-Hyderabad, diffusion models estimating dynamics for adaptive control, and now perceptive locomotion for the Unitree R1 humanoid with Prof. Ye Zhao at Georgia Tech.

The question I keep returning to: how do we train policies that are robust enough to survive the gap between simulation and the real robot?
