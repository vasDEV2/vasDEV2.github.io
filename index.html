V R Vasudevan

Graduate Researcher at LIDAR Lab, advised by Prof. Ye Zhao

I am a Masters student in Robotics at Georgia Tech. My interest lie in ensuring generalizable robot behavior across diverse environments and under uncertainty through robot learning. Currently, my work involves training a multitude of skills for humanoids (Unitree G1), later to be chained together to form one versatile locomotion policy. In addition, I am also working on improving the generalization capabilities of Vision Language Action models through data augmentation with synthetic data for manipulation.

I did my undergrad at Delhi Technological Univerisity where I was part of Team UAS-DTU. I built planning and control algorithms for fixed-wings and multirotors, with work spanning from collision avoidance to target following. Over three and a half years with UAS-DTU, I went from Airframe engineer and team pilot to Avionics Head, securing podium finishes at three international competitions, including the DARPA Triage Challenge. I've since trained RL quadrotor controllers at IIIT-Hyderabad and also worked on GNSS-denied localization at EPFL.
